## Foumula lui Gauss

Suma unui sir de termeni consecutivi se calculeaza astfel:  

$\dfrac{(\textit{primul}+\textit{ultimul})\cdot\textit{nr de termeni}}{2}$  
unde

$\boxed{\textit{nr de termeni}}$ se calculeaza ca $\boxed{\textit{ultimul}-\textit{primul}+\textit{1.}}$
